Price signal and company news

News sentiment is an experimental input. Positive headlines do not guarantee gains, and negative headlines do not guarantee losses. The agent may classify an opinion headline as positive or negative; that does not establish that a deal happened.

Experimental validation

Forward validation scores the first saved forecast for each stock, target session and history window against the actual completed close. It compares the combined model with a no-change baseline. Direction accuracy is measured on those saved results; headline-classification accuracy is not stock-prediction accuracy.

How estimates are made

The price baseline uses historical daily log returns, with the average shrunk toward zero. Recent company-headline sentiment contributes a capped adjustment of at most ±0.5 percentage points of log return. The adjustment is experimental and has not been fitted to stock-price outcomes.

The displayed range is an approximate 80% interval under a volatility model, not a promise of 80% real-world accuracy. Earnings surprises, incomplete sources and market-wide events can move prices outside it. Small history windows are especially uncertain. These are experimental estimates, not trading instructions.

News model: DistilRoBERTa financial sentiment. Method background: forecast intervals and time-series validation.
